Controls

Mouse
Turn the putter around the ball. Hold Shift for fine moves, or use AD.
Hold
Press and hold the left button, pull the mouse back toward you to take the putter back, release to putt. A longer backswing hits it firmer.
←→↑↓
Turn your head. Right-drag and the mouse wheel work too. C centres your view.
Esc
Cancel a backswing (right-click works too). M opens this menu.
Space
Hold while the ball rolls to speed it up.
Touch
Drag one finger to turn the putter, two fingers to look around, and use the pad at the bottom right to putt.
